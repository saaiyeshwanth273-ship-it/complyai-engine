# ComplyAI Regulatory Taxonomy

## 1. India First Architecture

### A. Central Legislation & Regulatory Domains
- **Constitutional:** Constitution of India, Fundamental Rights, Article 19(1)(g), Directive Principles.
- **Corporate & Commercial:** Companies Act 2013, LLP Act 2008, Indian Contract Act 1872, IBC 2016, Competition Act 2002.
- **Labour & Employment:**
  - *New Central Codes:* Code on Wages 2019, Industrial Relations Code 2020, Code on Social Security 2020, OSHWC Code 2020.
  - *Legacy & Active Statutes:* Minimum Wages Act 1948, Payment of Wages Act 1936, EPF & MP Act 1952, ESI Act 1948, Payment of Gratuity Act 1972, POSH Act 2013, Contract Labour Act 1970, Factories Act 1948.
- **Taxation:**
  - *Direct Tax:* Income-tax Act 1961, Income-tax Rules, Finance Acts, CBDT Circulars, TDS/TCS, Transfer Pricing.
  - *Indirect Tax:* CGST Act 2017, SGST Acts, IGST Act, GST Rules, e-Invoicing, e-Way bills.
- **Privacy & Data Protection:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA), Information Technology Act 2000 & IT Rules.
- **Cybersecurity:** CERT-In Cyber Security Directions (Section 70B(6)), NCIIPC Critical Information Infrastructure regulations.
- **Consumer Protection:** Consumer Protection Act 2019, E-Commerce Rules, Product Liability.
- **Environmental & ESG:** Environment (Protection) Act 1986, Water Act, Air Act, CPCB / SPCB E-Waste & Plastic Waste Rules.

### B. State-Level Architecture
Every state has independent jurisdiction modules:
`India:Karnataka`, `India:Maharashtra`, `India:Tamil_Nadu`, `India:Delhi`, `India:Telangana`, `India:Gujarat`, `India:Uttar_Pradesh`, `India:West_Bengal`, etc.
Covering:
- Shops and Commercial Establishments Acts
- State Professional Tax Acts
- State Labour Rules & Minimum Wage Notifications
- State Pollution Control Board requirements

## 2. International Jurisdictions

### A. European Union
- EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679)
- EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)
- NIS2 Directive (Tracking national transposing laws)
- EDPB Guidelines & CJEU Precedents

### B. United States
- **Federal:** Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), ADA, FTC Act Section 5, HIPAA, SOX.
- **State:** California CCPA/CPRA, Virginia VCDPA, Colorado Privacy Act, New York SHIELD.

### C. United Kingdom
- UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, Equality Act 2010, Companies Act 2006.

### D. Singapore
- Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA), Employment Act, Workplace Fairness Legislation.

### E. Australia
- Privacy Act 1988 (Australian Privacy Principles), Fair Work Act 2009, Corporations Act 2001.

### F. Canada
- PIPEDA (Federal), Provincial Privacy Acts (Quebec Law 25, Alberta PIPA, BC PIPA).

### G. UAE
- Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (Mainland Data Protection).
- Free Zones: DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020, ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021.

### H. International Standards & Frameworks
- **ILO:** Fundamental Conventions (C138 Minimum Age, C182 Worst Forms of Child Labour) vs non-binding Recommendations.
- **OECD:** Model Tax Convention, Pillar Two (GloBE Model Rules), OECD AI Principles.
